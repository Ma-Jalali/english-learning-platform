Scene 01 · Notice

One test becomes four purposeful exchanges.

IELTS does not measure one vague idea of “good English”. It gathers evidence from four skills: how you understand written and spoken language, and how clearly you communicate in writing and speech.

Academic and General Training candidates share Listening and Speaking. Reading and Writing change because the context and communication purpose change.

Why separate skill bands?

A separate band reveals a profile. You may follow a complex article confidently while needing more control when developing an extended spoken idea.

Scene 02 · Connect

What IELTS measures

Each task creates a situation in which a skill becomes visible. Strong performance combines task understanding with language ability—it is not a collection of tricks.

Locate ideas, distinguish claims, follow relationships and interpret detail in context.
Follow purpose, detail, attitude and development across conversations and talks.
Address the task, organise ideas, use vocabulary precisely and control grammar.
Develop ideas fluently, choose language flexibly and remain intelligible.
↗

Exam tip

Treat instructions as the opening line of the conversation.

“NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER” tells you exactly what kind of response the task can accept. Listen or scan with that boundary already in mind.
