# Product decisions

## Curriculum governance

The school owns pre-designed courses. Administrator/academic-designer content is locked for teachers.

Teacher additions exist only in separate empty slots and are visibly labelled as teacher additions. A teacher can modify or delete only their own addition; they cannot alter the official lesson, name, branding, quiz, assessment or navigation.

## First pilot

**B1 Preliminary Writing and Speaking mini-course.**

It tests a normal lesson, original Cambridge-style practice, writing feedback, speaking evidence, portfolio workflow and teacher cohort delivery.

## Free first, paid later

The design/pilot release is free. Paid sales later need two separate routes:

- USD and a compliant international payment provider for international learners.
- Iranian toman and an eligible Iranian payment gateway for Iranian learners.
